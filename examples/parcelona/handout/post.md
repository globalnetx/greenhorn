# First post in #ask-the-ai

We have a new colleague, and it is an AI, not a person. It is called
Estiba, and Lena and I have spent the last few weeks teaching it our
services, our terms, and our way of writing. Try it today on something
real. Paste a customer's email and say "ask me what you need to know,
then draft the reply", and see what comes back. When you are not sure
how to ask, ask it: "how should I brief you for this?"

The ground rules are four lines in the handout in your inbox, and worth
the minute. This channel is for the rest: post what worked, with the
ask you typed, and post what it got wrong. The second one is the useful
one. Every wrong answer that lands here becomes a fix for everyone.

# Parcelona Customer Guide (extract)

## 2. How a shipment moves

**Quote.** You send us the goods, the volume, and the pick-up and delivery
addresses. We reply with a quote within one working day for routes we
serve regularly, and within three working days for new routes. Each quote
has a reference starting QT- and states how long it is valid. Sea freight
quotes are usually valid for 14 days, air freight quotes for 7 days,
because carriers change their prices often.

**Booking.** When you accept a quote, we confirm the booking within one
working day and send you a booking number starting BK-. From then on,
please quote that number in every message.

**Collection and departure.** We arrange collection at origin through our
partner agent and tell you the planned departure. The departure date
depends on the carrier's schedule and on the cargo reaching the port
before the cut-off.

**Transit.** You can follow each container or consignment online with its
shipment number, which starts SH-. Arrival dates shown are the carrier's
estimates.

**Customs.** For goods arriving in Spain, we clear customs as your
representative once we have the commercial invoice, the packing list, and
any certificates the goods need. Clearance usually takes one to two
working days after the goods arrive, unless customs selects the shipment
for inspection.

**Delivery and invoice.** We deliver to the address on the booking and
send you the proof of delivery. Our invoice follows within five working
days. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date unless your rate
agreement says otherwise.

## 5. Delays and claims

We tell you about any delay we learn of on the same working day, with the
reason and the new estimate as soon as the carrier gives one.

If goods arrive damaged or incomplete, please note it on the delivery
receipt and tell us in writing within 7 days of delivery, with photos.
We open a claim, give you a claim number starting CL-, and handle it with
the carrier and the insurer. Our liability is set out in our terms of
business.

## 6. Contact and escalation

Your customer coordinator is your first contact for everything. We answer
emails within four working hours, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00
Central European Time.

If you are not happy with how something is handled, ask your coordinator
to pass it to the operations team lead. If it is still not resolved,
write to the managing director at the address on our website.

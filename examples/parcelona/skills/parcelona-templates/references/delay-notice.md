# Delay notice to a customer

**Subject:** [Shipment reference] from [origin port]: new estimated arrival

Hi [first name, as the customer signs],

The vessel carrying [shipment reference] ([container count and type],
[origin port] to [destination port]) is now expected to arrive on
[new date], instead of [original date]. This is the carrier's current
estimate; we will confirm the date once the vessel has berthed.

[One sentence on the reason, only as stated by the carrier. Nothing
assumed.]

What this means for you: [the consequence that matters to this
customer: a later delivery window, a changed customs slot, storage
days]. [What Parcelona is doing about it, if anything.]

We will send the next update [when: on berthing, or on a given date].
If the new date causes a problem on your side, reply to this message or
call [coordinator role] on the usual number and we will look at options
together.

[Sender's first name], Parcelona [office]

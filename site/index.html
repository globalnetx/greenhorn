Getting started

Before you start. Greenhorn is built for companies that have already said yes to AI. If yours hasn't decided yet, start there. Think privacy through before the first document goes in, and only give Greenhorn documents and information your company allows you to share with an AI. The full note.

Three steps. After that, your AI takes you by the hand.

  1. Install Greenhorn, for yourself only. Not for the whole organisation. Here's how.
  2. Make an empty folder somewhere. Open it in the Code tab of Claude Desktop.
  3. Type /greenhorn:giddy-up. From there it's a conversation. It asks, you answer, it writes things down, and then it tells you in plain words what it wrote.

You'll need Claude Desktop with the Code tab, Python 3.9 or newer, permission to set shared instructions for your team's AI, your website address, and whatever you've got lying around: a brand guide, a handbook, a few emails you're proud of. No need to know your colour codes or font names. Greenhorn works those out with you.

The tack room · one icon per command

Greenhorn speaks ranch.

Every command starts with greenhorn: because that's how Claude Code shows commands from a plugin. You don't have to remember any of them. Say what you want and the right one runs.

/greenhorn:giddy-up

The first run. Reads your website, takes your documents, interviews you, builds the first version.

Or just say: "set up our assistant"

/greenhorn:wrangle

Adds a skill, or fixes one. A lesson, a document, or "it keeps getting X wrong" goes in; a skill comes out.

Or just say: "add a skill", "capture this"

/greenhorn:reins

Tidies up the routing: the always-on block, and the table of which skill to open for what. Runs by itself after a skill is added.

Or just say: "it isn't using the glossary"

/greenhorn:round-up

Checks the folder: broken pointers, overlapping skills, stale references, claims with no source. Also runs before every build.

Or just say: "check everything", "anything stale?"

/greenhorn:roll-out

Packs everything up for your team's AI (a plugin, for Claude) and tells you exactly how to get it to them.

Or just say: "publish this", "push it to the team"

/greenhorn:campfire

Tells your team about their new colleague, in the form you want: an email, a post in your channel, a short note, or a handout. In your voice, with your own examples, and a named person to ask.

Or just say: "announce it to the team"

/greenhorn:homestead

Optional, for later. Puts the folder under version control, in a private repository, so a colleague can help maintain it.

Or just say: "put this on GitHub"

The first run is the interview plus every other command, once. Your AI tells you that too, so you know the others are there.

We didn't invent any of this. Greenhorn is a bundle of habits we picked up at GNX while getting our own AI to work for us. Write down what a good colleague would know. Keep each rule in one place. Check it before it goes out. Tell people how to use it. What we've done is put it all in one package that does the work with you.

# Meet our new colleague

[Company name], [month and year]

## A note from [advocate's first name]

[The admin's own words, four to six sentences, first person: we have a

Then one line handing over to the next section: "I asked it to

Signed with the advocate's first name and role.]

## Hello from [the assistant's name, when the persona skill has one; else "your new colleague"]

*Written by the assistant itself.*

[Four paragraphs in the first person, written by the assistant from
the persona skill, plain that it is an AI and not a person, with no
human name and no invented feelings or history. The persona's introduction
paragraph opens the section as written. One: who I am and what I was taught, as things a colleague
would notice: what we are and are not, the unwritten rules everyone
learns in week one, our reference formats, how we write, who owns what,
our look. Two: what I am here for, from the day-one list and the
first-month uses: I draft, you read, change, and send under your name;
what I can reach in our systems, if anything is connected, with your
own permissions. Three: how to brief me, the five parts in a sentence,
then the three or four habits that matter most here, never the whole
list (hand me the document; when you are not sure how to ask, ask me;
correct me once, then start fresh), and that I check with you first,
in the conduct skill's wording of the confirm rule, since those are
the rules we agreed. Four: what I do not know (your inbox, this morning's call,
anything after I was last taught) and, only when the admin wants it in
and the profile has a case, one thing I got wrong and which rule now
stops it; when I get something wrong, tell [the advocate], that is how
I get better.]

## The ground rules

Using it is encouraged. The rules say where the edges are, so you can
use it freely inside them. [One line on who agreed them and which policy
they sit under.]

- **Never into an AI:** [the data classes from the house-rules skill, in
  plain words, one phrase each].
- **Our systems, only through the routes we provide:** [the sanctioned
  routes, one phrase each]. Never a script with your login, never your
  password in the conversation.
- **A person decides** before [the conduct skill's confirm list, in
  its own words; the same wording wherever the handout or a short form
  states this rule]. A draft you send yourself is yours to check.
- **It never pretends to be a person.** [One line on how open we are with
  customers about using AI.]

When it says no, it is doing what we asked it to. Tell it what you are really trying to get done and it will offer the
closest thing it can. If nothing covers it, that is a gap, and gaps are
how this grows: [post it in the channel or tell the advocate].
